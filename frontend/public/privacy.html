ClauseGuard ("we", "us") helps you understand contracts and terms. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices when you use our website and Android app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and, if provided, your name — used to sign in and store your contract analyses.
  • Contract content: the text or files you paste or upload for analysis, together with the analysis results.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (IP address, timestamps, quota counts) to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use it

To produce clause extraction, risk scoring, comparisons, and AI assistant answers about the documents you submit; to maintain your history; and to keep the service secure and within usage limits.

3. Sharing with third parties

To analyze and answer questions about your documents, the submitted text is sent to our AI model provider (Groq) over an encrypted connection. We use hosting providers for storage and compute. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

Analyses and account data are kept while your account is active. You can delete individual contracts, and may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is limited. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. No legal advice

ClauseGuard provides informational summaries and does not provide legal advice. Always consult a qualified professional for legal decisions.

7. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region).

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data. Contact us to exercise them.
